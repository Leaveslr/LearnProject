# Examples

## Example

A valuation factor appears profitable from 2018 to 2025. After adding delisted stocks, realistic rebalancing dates, and 20 bps round-trip cost, the return drops sharply.

## Counterexample

A factor with lower headline return but lower turnover and stable behavior across regimes may be more useful than a high-return factor that collapses after costs.

## Exercise

Given a factor report, ask:

- Was the historical universe point-in-time?
- Were features available before the rebalance date?
- What is monthly turnover?
- What happens after realistic fees and slippage?
