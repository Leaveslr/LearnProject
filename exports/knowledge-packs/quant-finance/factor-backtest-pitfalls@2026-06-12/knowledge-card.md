# Knowledge Card

## What It Is

Single-factor backtest pitfalls are the common ways a promising research result becomes misleading before it reaches real trading.

## Why It Matters

Beginners often treat a high return curve as proof that a factor works. In practice, the curve may come from data leakage, survivorship bias, excessive turnover, or overfitting.

## Core Mechanism

The backtest answers only one question: "Did this rule work on this historical sample under these assumptions?" It does not automatically answer whether the rule can survive new data, realistic costs, and execution constraints.

## Boundaries

This pack explains research hygiene and content framing. It does not provide investment advice, a tradable strategy, or a performance guarantee.
