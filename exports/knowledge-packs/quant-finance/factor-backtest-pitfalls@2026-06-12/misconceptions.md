# Misconceptions

## Misconception 1

"The return curve is smooth, so the factor is reliable."

Correction: Smoothness can be produced by leakage, smoothing assumptions, or a narrow sample.

## Misconception 2

"If the factor is statistically significant, it is tradable."

Correction: Statistical signal does not include costs, capacity, risk, or execution.

## Misconception 3

"Adding more filters always improves the strategy."

Correction: More filters can quietly overfit the historical sample.
