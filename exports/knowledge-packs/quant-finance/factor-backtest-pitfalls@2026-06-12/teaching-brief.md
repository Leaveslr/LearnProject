# Teaching Brief

## Recommended Opening

Start with the moment a beginner sees a beautiful equity curve and thinks the strategy is solved.

## Must Explain

- A backtest is an experiment, not a money printer.
- Data leakage means the model saw information it would not have had live.
- Survivorship bias changes the historical sample.
- Turnover converts paper alpha into real costs.
- Robustness checks are part of the strategy, not optional decoration.

## Helpful Analogy

A backtest is like a practice exam. A high score matters only if the exam conditions match the real test.

## Avoid

- Do not imply all factors are useless.
- Do not present one checklist as sufficient for production trading.
- Do not use guaranteed-return language.
